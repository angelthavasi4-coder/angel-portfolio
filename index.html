How a deal moves with me

Six steps, from the first conversation to the next renewal. The last step feeds the first one again.

  1. 1

    Find the lead

    Calls, LinkedIn, email and renewal lists, tracked in CRM, Sales Navigator and Apollo.

  2. 2

    Understand the need

    Requirement gathering with IT managers, CIOs and CISOs, before any product comes up.

  3. 3

    Shape the solution

    Technical teams, distributors and partners help build the technical and commercial proposal.

  4. 4

    Quote and negotiate

    Clear quotations, RFQ and tender responses, and timely commercial follow-ups.

  5. 5

    Close and stay close

    Closure, then regular communication through onboarding and support.

  6. 6

    Renew and expand

    Renewals, upsell and cross-sell, led by regular check-ins with the customer.
